
# Activity 1 – Presentation Design Principles

## Activity Description

This activity focused on applying fundamental presentation and graphic design principles through the creation of a promotional poster for **Coffee For All**, a free coffee initiative for students. The project involved organizing visual elements and creating an appealing design that communicates the purpose of the activity.

## What Have I Learned?

- I learned the importance of visual hierarchy, alignment, contrast, balance, and color coordination in creating effective designs.
- I developed skills in organizing text and images to make information clear and visually appealing.
- One challenge was arranging the different design elements without making the poster look overcrowded. I addressed this by adjusting their placement and emphasizing the most important information.
- In the future, I can apply these principles to academic presentations, health education materials, and promotional designs. I also hope to improve my skills in typography and layout.
