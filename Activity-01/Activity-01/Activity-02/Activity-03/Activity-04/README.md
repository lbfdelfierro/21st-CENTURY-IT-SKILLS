
# Activity 4 – Google Meet and Google Forms

## Activity Description

This activity explored the use of Google Meet and Google Forms as digital tools for communication, collaboration, assessment, and information gathering. Through a group presentation and quiz activity, we demonstrated how these platforms can support online learning and interactive participation.

## What Have I Learned?

- I learned how Google Meet facilitates virtual communication and collaboration, while Google Forms can be used to create quizzes, surveys, and assessments.
- I developed skills in using digital platforms, organizing information, presenting content, and facilitating online activities.
- One challenge was ensuring that the presentation and online activities were organized and easy for participants to follow. This was addressed through preparation, coordination, and familiarization with the platforms.
- In the future, I can apply these tools to group projects, online discussions, academic assessments, and professional communication in nursing.

## Activity Outputs

- Group Presentation on Google Meet and Google Forms
- Google Forms Quiz
- Quiz Scores
