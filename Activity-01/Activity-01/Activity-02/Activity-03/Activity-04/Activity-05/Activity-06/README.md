
# Activity 6 – Making a Website

## Activity Description

This activity involved developing a website concept for **Louie's Cinnamon Rolls**, a small business idea inspired by my personal interest in baking. The website was designed to introduce the business, present its products, communicate its branding, and provide potential customers with relevant information.

## What Have I Learned?

- I learned how websites can be used to establish an online presence, promote products, and communicate information to potential customers.
- I developed skills in website planning, content organization, visual presentation, and the use of digital website-building tools.
- One challenge was organizing the website content while maintaining a consistent visual identity. I addressed this by considering the business concept, branding, and overall presentation when arranging the content.
- In the future, I can apply these skills to develop websites for personal projects, small businesses, and other professional purposes. I would also like to explore more advanced website development features.
