
# Activity 7 – Resume Generation

## Activity Description

This activity explored the use of artificial intelligence tools in creating a professional and visually appealing resume. It involved developing an AI prompt to generate a one-page resume that presents personal information, educational background, skills, and relevant experiences in an organized and professional format.

## What Have I Learned?

- I learned how AI tools can assist in writing, organizing, and improving professional documents.
- I developed skills in prompt engineering, resume organization, content selection, and professional document presentation.
- One challenge was ensuring that the generated resume accurately represented my background while maintaining a clean and readable layout. I addressed this by reviewing the content and refining the instructions given to the AI tool.
- In the future, I can apply these skills when preparing professional documents, clinical applications, job applications, and other academic requirements. I also aim to improve my ability to evaluate and edit AI-generated content.
