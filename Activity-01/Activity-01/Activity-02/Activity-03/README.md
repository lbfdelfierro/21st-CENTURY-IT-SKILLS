
# Activity 3 – Social Media Infographics and Documentation

## Activity Description

This activity focused on creating an informative infographic for **SafeLine PH**, a proposed anonymous cyberbullying-reporting system designed to encourage students to speak up and seek assistance regarding online harassment. The infographic aimed to communicate an important social issue through clear, accessible, and visually engaging information.

## What Have I Learned?

- I learned how infographics can simplify information and communicate important messages effectively.
- I developed skills in visual storytelling, information organization, layout design, and digital communication.
- One challenge was presenting the information in a concise manner while ensuring that the message remained understandable and meaningful. I addressed this by organizing the content into sections and highlighting the most important points.
- In the future, I can apply these skills when creating health education materials, awareness campaigns, and informational resources for patients and communities.
