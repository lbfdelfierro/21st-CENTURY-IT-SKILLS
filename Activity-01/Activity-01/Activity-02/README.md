
# Activity 2 – Color Palette and Typography

## Activity Description

This activity involved developing a visual brand identity for **Louie's Cinnamon Rolls**, a business concept inspired by my interest in baking. The project included creating a logo, selecting a suitable color palette, choosing typography, and designing a business card to establish a consistent brand identity.

## What Have I Learned?

- I learned how colors and typography influence the personality and overall identity of a brand.
- I developed skills in logo creation, color selection, visual consistency, and basic branding.
- One challenge was choosing colors and fonts that complemented each other while representing the warm and welcoming concept of a cinnamon roll business. I addressed this by exploring different combinations and selecting elements that worked well together.
- In the future, I can apply these skills to actual business branding, product packaging, promotional materials, and other creative projects. I would also like to explore more advanced graphic design techniques.

## Activity Output

Louie's Cinnamon Rolls Logo, Color Palette, Typography, and Business Card
